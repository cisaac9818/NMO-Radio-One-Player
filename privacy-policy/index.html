Website and artist submissions

This section applies to the NMotion website, station players and artist submission pages. The existing mobile application policy is reproduced below and applies to those applications, not to information you voluntarily send through this website’s submission workflow.

Information you choose to provide

The video submission and broadcast consent pages ask for contact details, artist and video information, a review or download link, rights confirmations and, for a release, a typed signature and date. Fields are processed in your browser to prepare an email and a downloadable copy. These pages do not automatically upload a video, submit the form to a database or send an email. Information is sent when you send the prepared email or otherwise contact us.

Use and handling of submissions

We use received information to evaluate submissions, contact you, confirm permissions, schedule approved content and retain evidence of broadcast authorization. Do not send passwords, identity documents or financial details. Use a shareable video link instead of a large email attachment; ensure your link permissions permit NMotion to review it.

Your email provider and NMotion’s email provider process messages and attachments. Website hosting and streaming providers may process technical connection data. Access to received submission and release records should be limited to those handling review, scheduling and rights administration. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion; some records may need to be retained for continuing licenses, legal obligations or disputes.

Station players and third parties

Opening a station player connects to nmotvtoo.com, FastCast4u or Everest Cast. Direct station pages may load their player when opened. Providers receive connection information such as IP address and browser details and may use cookies or browser storage. Their own privacy policies apply. External links may lead to services with different practices.

Cookies, advertising and downloads

NMotion has not added website analytics or advertising-tracking scripts to these pages. Hosting services, embedded players, mobile applications and third-party sites may have their own logging, cookies or advertising technologies. Downloaded submission or consent copies remain on your device; protect or remove them when appropriate.

Children and submissions

Artist submission and consent forms are for adults aged 18 or over, including authorized adult representatives. Do not enter a minor’s personal contact details in these forms. For questions about submissions involving minors, contact us before proceeding.

Questions and changes

Email nmotion315@gmail.com for privacy requests. Updates to the website notice will be dated on this page.
